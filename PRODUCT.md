# BuyByFit

## Users

People furnishing a real room who want to describe their needs naturally, then compare furniture before leaving for a retailer.

## Product purpose

BuyByFit is a local-data furniture discovery MVP. Visitors begin with a free-form room brief, typed or dictated in compatible browsers; the demo then reveals the same mock catalogue for every query while an AI recommendation layer is intentionally not yet connected.

## Principles

- Let furniture and material lead the page.
- Let visitors express a room's mood and constraints in their own language.
- Offer voice dictation as an optional input method without making microphone access required.
- Show constraints plainly: dimensions, price, style, and retailer availability.
- Keep the browsing flow calm, quick to scan, and useful on a phone.
